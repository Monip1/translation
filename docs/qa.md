href="#a3-presentation-and-qa" class="anchor-heading" aria-labelledby="a3-presentation-and-qa"><svg viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true"><use xlink:href="#svg-link"></use></svg></a> A3 Presentation and Q&amp;A </h2> <p>Your A3 slot at your team meeting is 20 minutes. The first 10 minutes are your presentation. The last 10 minutes are Q&amp;A with your tutor. This page gives a plan for the presentation and explains how the Q&amp;A works.</p> <p>The Q&amp;A checks that you understand your own work. The code your agent wrote is still your code, so you must be able to explain it.</p> <h3 id="presentation-guidelines"> <a href="#presentation-guidelines" class="anchor-heading" aria-labelledby="presentation-guidelines"><svg viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true"><use xlink:href="#svg-link"></use></svg></a> Presentation guidelines </h3> <p>We grade how clearly you explain your work and your design decisions. We do not grade how hard or complex your code is. A simple change that you explain well is better than a hard change that you cannot explain. Both partners present.</p> <p>A plan you can follow (10 minutes):</p> <ol> <li><strong>Purpose (1 min).</strong> What does the feature do? Who uses it?</li> <li><strong>Where the code is (2 min).</strong> Which files, classes, and functions matter? Show them in a simple diagram. Do not show a lot of code.</li> <li><strong>How it works (2 min).</strong> Walk through what happens from the user’s action to the result. Say what each arrow means and what happens in each function call.</li> <li><strong>Your choices (2 min).</strong> What did you decide and why? What other options did you think about? What would you do differently?</li> <li><strong>Working with the agent (2 min).</strong> How did you direct the agent? How did you check its work? Show one example of a suggestion you accepted, changed, or rejected, and why.</li> <li><strong>How you worked together (1 min).</strong> How did you split the driver and navigator roles? What did you learn from working that way? What surprised you?</li> </ol> <p><strong>Diagrams.</strong> A simple sequence diagram is a good way to show how the feature works. A sketch with boxes and arrows for the files, classes, and functions also works. You can use a UML class diagram if it helps, but you do not have to.</p> <p>Tips:</p> <ul> <li>Show a demo if you can. It is the fastest way to show what your feature does.</li> <li>Show only the small parts of code that you need. Do not read code line by line.</li> <li>Each partner should talk about a part they know well. Learn your partner’s part too, because the Q&amp;A can ask either of you about any part.</li> <li>Practice once with a timer.</li> </ul> <h3 id="how-the-qa-works"> <a href="#how-the-qa-works" class="anchor-heading" aria-labelledby="how-the-qa-works"><svg viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true"><use xlink:href="#svg-link"></use></svg></a> How the Q&amp;A works </h3> <ul> <li>Your tutor asks a question to one specific student, and that student answers. Your partner cannot answer for you.</li> <li>Both partners must be ready to answer questions about any part of the work. Do not split the work into “my part” and “your part” and learn only one.</li> <li>Your tutor may pick any part of your process journal or design document and ask you to explain it in detail.</li> <li>Your tutor may follow up on your answer, for example by asking you to show the code or explain a different option.</li> </ul> <h3 id="what-you-may-use"> <a href="#what-you-may-use" class="anchor-heading" aria-labelledby="what-you-may-use"><svg viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true"><use xlink:href="#svg-link"></use></svg></a> What you may use </h3> <ul> <li>You may open your repository, code, tests, process journal, and design document.</li> <li>You may use notes that you prepared beforehand.</li> <li>You may not use an AI agent or any AI chat tool during the Q&amp;A.</li> </ul> <h3 id="what-your-tutor-may-ask-about"> <a href="#what-your-tutor-may-ask-about" class="anchor-heading" aria-labelledby="what-your-tutor-may-ask-about"><svg viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true"><use xlink:href="#svg-link"></use></svg></a> What your tutor may ask about </h3> <p>These are examples. Your tutor may ask these questions or others like them.</p> <p><strong>Design decisions</strong></p> <ul> <li>What options did you consider for this decision? What are the tradeoffs?</li> <li>Why did you choose this option? Who made the decision, you, your partner, or the agent?</li> <li>Why did you choose the languages you support? How would someone add another language, and what would break or need to change?</li> <li>How does your design fit the conventions of the existing code?</li> </ul> <p><strong>Code</strong></p> <ul> <li>Walk me through what happens from the moment the user starts the operation to the moment the new column appears.</li> <li>What does this function do? What happens if its input is empty, or if it fails?</li> <li>Why is this code in this file or class and not somewhere else?</li> <li>Here is a line the agent wrote. Explain what it does and why it is needed.</li> </ul> <p><strong>Tests</strong></p> <ul> <li>What does this test check? Why does it matter?</li> <li>Could this test fail? What change to the code would make it fail?</li> <li>Which edge cases did you test? Which did you decide not to handle, and why?</li> <li>How did you test failure handling? How did you know the tests were testing the right behavior?</li> </ul> <p><strong>Process journal and design document</strong></p> <ul> <li>Explain this paragraph or diagram in detail. Does it match what you built?</li> <li>You wrote that you checked this. What exactly did you do?</li> <li>Your journal says the agent suggested this. How did you decide whether to accept it?</li> </ul> <h3 id="if-you-cannot-answer"> <a href="#if-you-cannot-answer" class="anchor-heading" aria-labelledby="if-you-cannot-answer"><svg viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true"><use xlink:href="#svg-link"></use></svg></a> If you cannot answer </h3> <p>It is fine to say “I do not know”. Say what you do know and how you would find the answer. Your tutor may rephrase the question or give a hint. A single gap will not hurt your score by itself, but repeated gaps will.</p> <p>Your tutor asks each student separately, so partners can receive different scores.</p> <h3 id="grading"> <a href="#grading" class="anchor-heading" aria-labelledby="grading"><svg viewBox="0 0 16 16" aria-hidden="true"><use xlink:href="#svg-link"></use></svg></a> Grading </h3> <p>The Q&amp;A counts toward your <strong>Technical</strong> and <strong>Communication</strong> scores. Your answers show whether you understand your design, code, and tests. They also show how clearly you explain them. See the <a href="/assignments/a3/rubric">A3 Rubric</a> for the grading rubric.</p> </main> <hr> <footer> <p class="text-small mb-0">Copyright &copy; 2026 Gerald Soosairaj.
